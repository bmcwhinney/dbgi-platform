A newly launched founder grant fund has committed 150,000 XCD to its first cohort, offering non-dilutive capital to early-stage ventures that have a working prototype but have not yet raised institutional funding. The fund's organisers say the goal is to fill a gap between friends-and-family capital and the seed rounds typically written by regional accelerators.

Grants range from 5,000 to 20,000 XCD and come with light-touch reporting requirements rather than board seats or equity stakes. Applicants must demonstrate island-based operations and a plan to hire locally as the business scales.

The fund's first cohort includes ventures in agritech, logistics, and consumer products. Organisers say a second funding round is planned for early next year, contingent on securing additional commitments from private and institutional donors.
