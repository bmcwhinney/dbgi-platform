Every Thursday afternoon, a rotating group of mentors sets up in the accelerator's shared space for a session anyone with a business idea can walk into, no application, no pitch deck required. The format is deliberately informal: a conversation about the idea, a few pointed questions, and, if it seems warranted, an introduction to someone further along a similar path.

Organisers say the goal is to lower the barrier to a first conversation for people who might not yet see themselves as founders. Recent sessions have covered ideas ranging from a mobile repair cooperative to a proposed expansion of the sea moss supply chain into cosmetics.

Attendance has grown steadily since the sessions launched, and organisers are now considering a satellite version in Portsmouth to reach founders outside the capital.
