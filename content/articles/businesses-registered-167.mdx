The companies registry recorded 167 new business registrations this quarter, a 19 percent increase on the same period last year. The growth was broad-based rather than concentrated in a single sector, though services and light manufacturing accounted for the largest share of new filings.

Registry officials point to a simplified online filing process introduced earlier this year as part of the explanation, along with a general uptick in confidence following the geothermal plant's first power delivery. Several of the new registrations are formalisations of businesses that had operated informally for years, suggesting the headline number understates the underlying level of economic activity already taking place.

The registry has not yet published a sector-by-sector breakdown, but plans to do so quarterly going forward, which should make it easier to track where the island's entrepreneurial energy is actually concentrated.
