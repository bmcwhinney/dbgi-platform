A round of regulatory modernisation completed over the past two years has put Roseau back on the radar for financial services firms evaluating regional bases, according to several advisors who work with institutions considering a presence on the island.

The changes touch licensing timelines, reporting requirements, and dispute resolution processes, areas that firms had previously cited as reasons to locate elsewhere in the region. Regulators say the overhaul was designed deliberately around feedback from firms that had considered and ultimately passed on the island in prior years.

It is early. No major institution has yet announced a full relocation, and advisors caution that regulatory reform alone rarely moves firms without complementary progress on talent availability and digital infrastructure. But the volume of exploratory inquiries has increased enough that it is now a regular topic among the small community of professionals who work on cross-border licensing in the region.
