Dominica's new international airport, under construction on the island's northeast coast near Wesley, is nearing the end of its most difficult phase: the runway itself. At 3,000 metres, the strip is being built long enough to take wide-body aircraft directly from North America, Europe, and beyond, a first for the island, whose current airport, Douglas-Charles, can't accommodate long-haul jets.

The footage above, filmed by Montreal Management Consultants, the project's managing contractor, shows the scale of the earthworks: a corridor cut straight through forested hills down to the Atlantic. Runway formation work is now more than 90 percent complete. The project, built by China Railway No. 5 and funded through Dominica's Citizenship by Investment programme, is on track for an opening in early 2028.
