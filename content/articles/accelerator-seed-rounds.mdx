Three ventures from the accelerator's most recent cohort have closed seed rounds, and for the first time, the lead investors in each round are based outside the region. The rounds range from an undisclosed amount to just under a million XCD-equivalent, according to people familiar with the deals.

Founders and accelerator staff describe the shift as evidence that the island's startup ecosystem has crossed a credibility threshold with outside capital, after several years of rounds being led almost exclusively by regional angels and family offices.

The three companies span fintech, logistics, and a consumer subscription product, suggesting international investor interest is not narrowly confined to a single category. Accelerator leadership say they expect the pattern to continue as more graduates reach the traction levels international funds typically require before engaging.
